# Release notes for sbmlsim 0.14.0
![sbmlsim](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/raw/develop/docs/images/favicon/sbmlsim-100x100-300dpi.png)

A release of the documentation, of quantities and of fits that converge. The documentation starts with a tutorial and shows what its code prints and plots. Results are quantities first: `res.q[...]`, `res.to(...)`, plots in units and `label_axes`. A fit simulates only the times of its data. Its finite differences follow the tolerance of the integrator, so the runs of a fit no longer stop next to their start values. A run of simulation experiments shows its progress and where the time went.

Results which change: the fits (another step of the finite differences), the Fisher information (another step), the units of SBML Level 1/2 models (the repressilator is in minutes and items) and the labels of figures (the short units of pint, `time [h]`).

## Features

- **A tutorial and the outputs of the documentation** ([#393](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/pull/393)).
  - **Tutorial.** `docs/tutorial.md` takes the whole body model of hydrochlorothiazide through the features of the package step by step: a simulation, observables, changes, scans, a sensitivity analysis, a fit to the data of Patel 1984 and an uncertainty analysis. The model is part of the package, `sbmlsim.resources.HCTZ_SBML`, so the tutorial runs after `pip install sbmlsim`.
  - **Outputs.** Every code block of the documentation is followed by what it prints and by the figures it draws. `scripts/docs_outputs.py` runs the blocks of a page and writes both into it. `tests/docs/test_docs_code.py` fails when a page is out of step with its code.
  - **Pages.** Every page was checked against the code and rewritten to be shorter and plainer.
- **Results are quantities first** ([#398](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/pull/398), [#400](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/pull/400), [#401](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/pull/401)).
  - `res.q["cp"]` is a variable or coordinate of a `ScanResult` as a quantity, e.g. `res.q["time"].to("hr")`. One point of a ragged result has no padding, so `res.sel(dose="high").q["cp"].max()` is its peak.
  - `res.to({"time": "hr", "pk.cmax": "µM"})` is a new result with variables and coordinates in other units and the same labels. Converting the time converts the times of the changes as well.
  - Quantities plot with matplotlib in their units: the first quantity on an axis sets its unit, the others are converted. `sbmlsim.plot.label_axes(ax, "time", "HCTZ plasma")` labels the axes `time [h]` and `HCTZ plasma [ng/ml]`.
  - `plot_bands` draws quantities with `time_unit`, `unit` and `name`, so a band shares an axis with lines of quantities. `plot_distribution` takes `unit` and `name`.
  - The table of the data points of a fit, `FitReport.points`, `FitMetrics.datapoints_df` and `datapoints.tsv`, has the units of `x` and of the values in the columns `x_unit` and `unit`.
  - `data.to_quantity` takes the unit registry of the package by default.
  - The documentation reads results as quantities, plots them with their units and sets no `steps`: the output of a simulation is the steps of the integrator, and a common grid where one is needed is `time=` of the run.
- **A run of experiments shows its progress and where the time went** ([#391](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/issues/391), [#399](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/pull/399)).
  - `ExperimentRunner.run_experiments(progress=None)` and `run_experiments` show a progress bar over the experiments and a row per running experiment on an interactive terminal or in a notebook, also for a run in a pool. `progress=False` prints nothing, `True` prints the last frame on any console.
  - A table of the timings ends the run: the seconds of every experiment per phase (models, initialization, simulations, data, figures, outputs) with totals and shares. `ExperimentResult.timings` has them.
  - The table fits narrow consoles and a console without UTF-8. The timing messages of the log are gone.

## Changes

- **Fits simulate the times of their data** ([#397](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/pull/397)).
  - A group of fit mappings simulates the start and the times of its data with the selections of its own mappings, also for observables which are timecourses without reductions, e.g. `Formula("cp", "[Cve_hctz] * Mr_hctz")`, which are evaluated on that output.
  - The evaluation of a fit, e.g. its report, simulates the output of the simulation for the curves of its figures, which were lines between the times of the data.
  - A step of the fit of the tutorial takes 1.0 ms instead of 2.0 ms.
- **The finite differences follow the tolerance of the integrator** ([#397](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/pull/397)).
  - **Before.** The least squares optimizer took the step of SciPy, the square root of the machine epsilon, far below the noise of the integrator: the jacobian was noise and every run of the HCTZ fit stopped next to its start values. The Fisher information took central differences with a step of `1e-6`, its standard errors were up to 30 times too small.
  - **Now.** A least squares fit takes `diff_step = sqrt(relative_tolerance)` of its settings (`1e-3`) unless it is given, also `fit_cli` (was `0.05`) and the re-optimizations of a profile (was `0.05`). The Fisher information takes the cube root of the relative tolerance (`1e-2`).
  - **Effect.** 17 of 20 runs of the HCTZ fit reach the best cost instead of 1 of 20, the median cost is 28.4 instead of 190.6. The Fisher matrix of its optimum is within `9e-4` of a reference at a tolerance of `1e-12`, it was off by a factor of 143.
- **The axis of a figure writes the short unit of pint**: `Axis("time", unit="hr")` is labelled `time [h]`, as `label_axes` labels it. A unit pint does not read is written as given.
- **Less output.** A `ScanResult` prints at most 8 of its variables. A plain number in the changes of a model logs no warning. A run without paths logs no warnings about them, and `ExperimentRunner` takes `base_path` and `data_path` as optional.

## Fixes

- The default units of SBML Level 1/2 models: the time is in `time`, a species in `substance` and a compartment in `volume`, `area` or `length`, each of them redefined by a unit definition of the same id. The repressilator was in seconds without units of its species.
- The PEtab reader gave the data of a concentration `[S]` the unit of the amount.
- A change of a model in a unit which cannot be converted raises a `ValueError`; an unknown target raises as well.
- `ExperimentReport` failed for an experiment without a source file, e.g. one defined in a notebook, and for a model of inline SBML.
- A figure without titles of its plots had an empty band below its title.
- A curve with a `style` warned about its label as an ignored style argument.
- The label of error bars reads `Patel 1984 ± SD`.
- `DataSet.from_df` warned about a `mean_sd` column which lost nothing.
- The label of a hypothesis of one scenario names its scenario, and units of hypotheses and of the plots of the analyses are short, `ng/ml`.
- `RoadrunnerSBMLModel.tolerances` gives the compartment of every species.
- The sodium and chloride urine data of Patel 1984 in `examples/hctz_fitting` are tagged as urine.
