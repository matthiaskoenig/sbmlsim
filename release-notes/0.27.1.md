# Release notes for sbmlsim 0.27.1
![sbmlsim](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/raw/develop/docs/images/favicon/sbmlsim-100x100-300dpi.png)

A rebuild of a model compares its web app without the hashes of its SBML, so the app of an unchanged model is not reported as changed, and the store of the jobs of the service is pruned before a job is recorded finished.

## Fixes

- **A rebuild compares the web app of a model without the sha256 of its SBML** ([#535](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/pull/535)). The app names the sha256 of the SBML of every run of its bundle, which the dates of the model history change, so a rebuild of an unchanged model reported its app as changed (the HCTZ model of pkdb_models). `same_app` compares two apps without these hashes and `Project.changed_outputs` uses it for `<name>_app.html`.
- **A finished job prunes the store before it is recorded finished** ([#534](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/pull/534)). The store of the jobs of the service recorded a job finished and only then dropped the finished jobs over `max_finished` with their directories, so a client which saw the job finished could still find the directory of a dropped job (a flaky test of the release 0.27.0); a job submitted first and finished last was dropped right after it finished, which it no longer is.
