# Release notes for sbmlsim 0.9.5
![sbmlsim](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/raw/develop/docs/images/favicon/sbmlsim-100x100-300dpi.png)

A patch release of the fixes which the port of the models of pkdb_models onto sbmlsim 0.9.4 found.

## Features

- **A legend outside widens only the gap right of its plot** (ruling 67 of the port of pkdb_models). `legend_position="outside"` added the width of a panel to every gap between the plots of a figure (`wspace + 1.0`), whether a plot had a legend or not, so the panels shrank, a legend could cover the labels of the plot right of it and the legend of the last column was cut at the edge of the figure; the band figures of the acetaminophen model of pkdb_models were made 72 inches wide to make room. A legend outside is now placed right of its plot together with the colour bars: its column gets the room of its widest legend, measured on the legend, the figure is that much wider and the panels keep their size, so a column without a legend gets no room. One legend per row is the legend of the last plot of every row (`legend=False` on the others), which widens the figure by the legend alone. The legend starts `0.12` inches right of the plot (or of its right y axis or its last colour bar) with its top at the top of the plot, and the title of a figure which became wider stays centered over its panels. The interactive figures of plotly keep their one legend of the figure right of all panels.

## Fixes

- **A tall figure has its plots right below its title** ([#312](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/issues/312)). The top of the plots was capped at the fraction of matplotlib (`0.88`), so for a figure taller than about 9.2 inches the gap between the title of the figure and the titles of its plots grew with the height: 1.2 inches above the plots for two rows, 3.6 inches for six rows (the RAAS figure of the losartan model of pkdb_models). The plots now begin below the band of the titles whatever the height; the gap between the title and the titles of the plots is 0.27 inches with the default sizes, and a figure of at most 9.2 inches does not change.
- **A heatmap of long labels is laid out** ([#314](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/issues/314)). `plot_heatmap` and the other heatmaps of `sbmlsim.sensitivity.plots` warned `constrained_layout not applied because axes sizes collapsed to zero` (14 times for the heatmap of the dulaglutide model of pkdb_models) when the labels of the parameters or of the observables were wider than the first figure, which was as wide as the cells and a margin, and the layout was not applied. The figure is now made as large as its labels before the first layout, measured on them, and an observable which is wider than its cell is rotated before the first layout; the sizes of the heatmaps with long labels change slightly.
