# Release notes for sbmlsim 0.18.1
![sbmlsim](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/raw/develop/docs/images/favicon/sbmlsim-100x100-300dpi.png)

A process which loads large models needs a fraction of the memory: the memory the compilation of a model freed goes back to the system, and the fit mappings of a project compile its models at its level.

## Fixes

- **The fit mappings compile at the level of the project** ([#476](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/pull/476)). `FitMappings` and `select_mapping_collections` take `jit_optimization`, and `FitMappings.for_project(project, experiment_classes)` creates the fit mappings of a project with its paths and its level (`SimulatorSettings.jit_optimization`), which `select_mappings` and the mapping table of the credibility (`check_declaration`) use. Before sbmlsim 0.18 libroadrunner ran the code of an earlier compilation at another level (sys-bio/roadrunner#1420), which hid that they compiled at `O2`: the declaration check of a model compiled at `O0` (creatinine of pkdb_models) needed 4.9 GB and about a minute per model.
- **The memory a compilation freed goes back to the system** ([#476](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/pull/476)). LLVM leaves about 0.7 GB of freed heap per large model, which glibc kept until the process ended. `sbmlsim.memory.release_freed_memory` returns it (`malloc_trim`) after every compilation of a model and after an experiment released its results; the declaration check above needs 1.7 GB instead of 3.9 GB at `O0`. Nothing happens without glibc (macOS, windows). Reported as sys-bio/roadrunner#1438.

`O2` stays the default level of the JIT; results do not change.
