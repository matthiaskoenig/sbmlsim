# Release notes for sbmlsim 0.44.0
![sbmlsim](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/raw/develop/docs/images/favicon/sbmlsim-100x100-300dpi.png)

The chart builder draws the scan figures of pkdb_models as matplotlib draws them, on sbmlsim-core 0.31.1: colour bars of colour maps given by stops with a mark at the default, fixed ticks, the markers of matplotlib, legends with titles and columns, and monospace text.

## Features

- **Colour scales from stops** (#676). Wherever a colour scale is taken (`ChartPlot.colorbar`, `heatmap`), a list of stops (position, colour), interpolated as matplotlib's `LinearSegmentedColormap.from_list`.
- **Colour bars** (#676): `marks`, lines across the bar at values; `placement` `right`, `top` (horizontal above the panels of the row) or `inset` (inside its panel at a legend location); `length` and `thickness`; tick labels of several lines.
- **Ticks** (#676). `ChartAxis(ticks=..., tick_labels=...)` fixes the ticks of an axis.
- **Markers** (#676). `MARKERS` gains the triangles down, left and right, star, filled x and plus, pentagon, hexagon and thin diamond, and `marker_fill_style` with `marker_fill_alt` draws the half fills of matplotlib; the figures of sbmlsim draw matplotlib's `v`, `<`, `>` and `*` with them, which were an upward triangle and a cross before.
- **Boxes and error bars** (#676): `box_width` and `error_width`.
- **Legends** (#676): `Chart.plot(legend_title=..., legend_columns=...)`; `Chart(legend_position="bottom", legend_columns=..., legend_title=...)`, the legend below the grid; `fonts` take `plot_title`.
- **Monospace text** (#676). `ChartPlot.text(..., family="monospace")`.

## Changes

- **sbmlsim-core 0.31.1** (#676). Its markers have the shapes of matplotlib (`diamond` is matplotlib's `D`), the decades of a log axis are written 10ⁿ and large or small values carry a multiplier.
