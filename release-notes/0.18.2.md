# Release notes for sbmlsim 0.18.2
![sbmlsim](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/raw/develop/docs/images/favicon/sbmlsim-100x100-300dpi.png)

Faster parallel scans with large results and a faster import, and a cleanup of unused and duplicated code. Results do not change.

## Features

- **The results of a pool scan travel through shared memory** ([#480](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/pull/480)). A worker writes an array of a chunk of at least 1 MiB into a segment of shared memory, which the parent reads without a copy (`sbmlsim.simulator.shared`), instead of pickling it through the pipe of the pool; no segment outlives a run, also after an error, Ctrl-C or a dead worker. A scan of the midazolam model over 2000 points with all 268 selections on a grid of 200 times (858 MB) takes 0.69 s instead of 0.95 s on 4 workers; the results are bitwise those of a serial run. Serial runs, small results and windows pickle as before. pymizu, a shared-memory pool, was compared and is not used: for results of 214 MB the stdlib shared memory is 2.6 times faster.
- **The unit registry is built from a disk cache** ([#479](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/pull/479)). The parsed definitions of pint are cached under `~/.cache/sbmlsim/units/` (`SBMLSIM_UNITS_CACHE`, empty for none), so `import sbmlsim` takes 0.36 s instead of 0.44 s in every process and every worker of a pool; an unusable cache falls back to the registry without it, and the registry is the same.
- **`Project.experiment_runner`** ([#478](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/pull/478)) initializes experiments with the paths and the JIT level of a project, as the project checks and `verify dump` do.

## Cleanup

- Unused functions and constants are removed ([#478](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/pull/478)): `data.load_pkdb_dataframes_by_substance`, `RoadrunnerSBMLModel.get_state_path`, `utils.md5_for_path`, `plotting.DEFAULT_COLORS`, `templates.BASE_TEMPLATE`, `testsuite.runner.FAILED_STATUS`; none of sbmlsim, pkpdsim or pkdb_models used them.
- The hashes of the run layer and the checksums of files are computed by `sbmlsim.hashing`, pinned by tests to the values before; the test suites of PEtab and SciML share the base of their case results; the tests run with `filterwarnings = error`.
