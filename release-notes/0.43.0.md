# Release notes for sbmlsim 0.43.0
![sbmlsim](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/raw/develop/docs/images/favicon/sbmlsim-100x100-300dpi.png)

The chart builder draws what the custom matplotlib figures of pkdb_models need, on sbmlsim-core 0.30.0, and a legend stays inside its plot where matplotlib would put it.

## Features

- **Legend locations** (#674). `legend_loc` of `FigureSettings`, `Chart` and `Chart.plot` places a legend inside at a location of matplotlib (`sbmlsim.plot.chart.LEGEND_LOCS`): `best`, the default, the location of the least data, or a fixed one such as `upper left`, which the renderer keeps whatever data the legend covers. A figure which sets `legend_loc` no longer warns that the renderer ignores it.
- **The fields of the renderer of sbmlsim-core 0.30.0 in the chart builder** (#674): `ChartAxis(line_visible=False)` hides the line of an axis; `Chart.plot(aspect="equal")` (or a positive number) makes a unit of y as long as a unit of x; `error_cap` of `ChartPlot.curve` and `ChartPlot.bar` draws the caps of the error bars; `ChartPlot.heatmap(..., x_edges=..., y_edges=...)` puts its cells between edges on numeric axes, so a space-time heatmap shares its time axis with a curve; `ChartPlot.text(size=..., weight="bold")`, and a text keeps its runs of spaces; a colour scale is any sequential or diverging map of matplotlib by its name in any case (`Blues`, `PuRd`, `RdBu_r`).

## Changes

- **sbmlsim-core 0.30.0** (#674, #673 took 0.29.1). Its renderer keeps a legend inside however much data it covers, as matplotlib does, and moves it right of the plot only when it does not fit into the panel; before, a legend covering more than 5 % of its frame with data went outside. The axis lines, ticks and tick labels are a darker grey (`#3b424d`). A grid of many thin columns or rows fits its figure.

## Fixes

- sbmlsim-core 0.29.1 no longer reports a stall at a step which leaves the time unchanged (#673).
