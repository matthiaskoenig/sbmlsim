# Release notes for sbmlsim 0.20.0
![sbmlsim](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/raw/develop/docs/images/favicon/sbmlsim-100x100-300dpi.png)

Correlated priors and the Laplace posterior of a fit as a source of the uncertainty analyses, the posterior in the fit report, faster defaults of the optimizers and faster evaluations of a fit.

Results which change: the defaults of the optimizers of a fit changed ([#496](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/pull/496)), so a fit with a seed draws other start values than before and reaches its optimum with fewer evaluations; the optima are the same within the tolerances of the fit. A fit with the old defaults takes `sampling=SamplingType.UNIFORM`, `ftol=xtol=gtol=1e-8` for least squares and `popsize=15`, `polish=True` for differential evolution. The Fisher information of a fit of the objective `likelihood` or `posterior` is the information of the data, without the residuals of the priors and without the factor `σ²` ([#492](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/pull/492)); a record of least squares is unchanged.

## Features

- **Correlated priors** ([#492](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/pull/492), [#190](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/issues/190)). `OptimizationProblem(prior_correlation=...)` and `FitDefinition.prior_correlation` take a `Covariance.correlation` of normal and log-normal priors by pid, a Gaussian copula of the priors restricted to their bounds. `sbmlsim.fit.priors.JointPrior` writes the priors as whitened residuals, so the cost of the objective `posterior` is the negative log posterior up to a constant. The correlation is pickled with the problem, stored with the result and written into the PEtab extension 0.5.0 (gap `prior-correlation`).
- **The Laplace posterior of a fit** ([#492](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/pull/492)). `sbmlsim.fit.posterior.posterior_laplace` is the normal of the precision `J'J / σ² + H_prior` in the space of the optimizer, reusing a given Fisher information; it raises for a direction neither the data nor the priors constrain and reports a parameter at a bound. A `ParameterPosterior` (`posterior.json`) gives its marginals, its correlations and an `UncertaintyModel`; `FitRun.posterior()`, and `posterior: true` of the kinds `fit` and `fisher` of the run layer. The record of an adopted fit keeps `posterior.json` and `posterior.tsv`.
- **The posterior and the priors as a source** ([#492](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/pull/492)). `ParameterPosterior.uncertainty_model()` for `propagate`, the design `posterior_parameters` of `sbmlsim.simulation.sampling`, and `prior_distributions(problem)`/`prior_model(problem)`, the priors truncated at the bounds as distributions and as an `UncertaintyModel` with their correlation.
- **The posterior in the fit report** ([#493](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/pull/493)). `FitReport(posterior=...)` adds the section **Posterior** (the credible intervals and the correlation of the parameters, `posterior.json`, `posterior.tsv`), the overview shows the priors and their correlation, and the report document gets the table `results:posterior` and the methods `methods:posterior`. The methods of the objectives `likelihood` and `posterior` describe the negative log-likelihood with the noise models of the training data and the truncated priors with their copula, instead of weighted least squares.
- **Correlated draws of the fitted parameters** ([#493](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/pull/493)). `AdoptedRecord` reads `fisher.json` and `posterior.json`, and `parameter_correlation` gives the dependence of the parameters of a record: the correlation of the posterior, else of a Fisher information of full rank, else independent. The experiments over the draws of the fitted parameters (`Project.run_experiments(uncertainty=...)`, the kind `simulate`) and the uncertainty of the credibility draw the distrib distributions of the SBML with that correlation.
- **Faster defaults of the optimizers** ([#496](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/pull/496)). Least squares from many start points stays the default of every fit, differential evolution is a global check of a final fit. The start values are a Latin hypercube on the logarithms of the bounds (`SamplingType.LOGUNIFORM_LHS`) in `run_optimization`, `OptimizationProblem.optimize` and `start_values`; least squares takes `ftol`, `xtol` and `gtol` from the relative tolerance of the integrator, at least `1e-8`; differential evolution takes `popsize=5` and is polished by least squares on the residuals (`polish="least_squares"`). On the HCTZ fits, least squares needs 2.5 to 2.8 times fewer evaluations per run which reaches the optimum and differential evolution about 4 times fewer.

## Performance

- **Less python per simulation** ([#494](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/pull/494)). The output grid of a segment is built without sorting, the restore of a model before its reset reads only the parameters and the compartments, and the integrator and the model of roadrunner are fetched once per initialization; an evaluation of a fit is about 5 % faster, the results are bitwise the same. A port of the simulation core to Rust was evaluated in [#227](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/issues/227) and not done: roadrunner integrates in compiled code for 87 to 93 % of a scan and about 60 % of an evaluation of a fit.
- **A fit simulates what its training data needs** ([#495](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/pull/495)). A group of a fit whose output is the times of its data ends at its last data point for a model without events (`Plan.until`) and selects only what its evaluated mappings read, so an optimization does not select the validation and outlier data; the residuals are bitwise the same and the curves of a report keep the whole simulation.

## Fixes

- The Fisher information of a fit of the objective `likelihood` or `posterior` has the covariance `(J'J)⁻¹` of the data with normal intervals and `n` the number of measurements; it scaled the covariance by `σ²` of the residuals of the likelihood and counted the priors as data ([#492](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/pull/492)). `FisherInformation.objective` records the objective.

## Dependencies

- The lock moves to the newest releases, among them sbmlutils 0.17.2, sbmlode 0.3.1, simrun 0.3.2, pandas 3.1.1, pydantic 2.14.3, ruff 0.17.4, ty 0.0.100 and zensical 0.1.0; the hooks of ruff and ty follow the locked versions, and the ignore comments of ty use its renamed rules.
