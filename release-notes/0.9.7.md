# Release notes for sbmlsim 0.9.7
![sbmlsim](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/raw/develop/docs/images/favicon/sbmlsim-100x100-300dpi.png)

A performance release: the constant parameters and the species of a model are read once per instance of roadrunner instead of on every call of `sampling.parameters_of`. The simulations are unchanged. The faster JIT of roadrunner, LLJIT, was measured and is not used, roadrunner 2.10 crashes with it (see below).

## Performance

- **`parameters_of` reads the SBML of a model once** ([#332](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/issues/332)). `sampling.parameters_of` serialized the SBML of the instance of roadrunner and parsed it again on every call to list the constant parameters and the species: 4.8 s of the 8.6 s of the initialization of the experiments of the acetaminophen model of pkdb_models (403 kB of SBML), which call it for 133 simulations of their sensitivity designs. The ids are now read once per instance of roadrunner and kept (`RoadrunnerSBMLModel.sbml_ids()`, an `SBMLIds`, read again for an instance which replaces `r`; an edit of the structure of the instance itself, e.g. `r.addParameter(...)`, which sbmlsim never makes, is not tracked). The initialization of the experiments of acetaminophen (the test `test_experiments_initialize` of pkdb_models) takes 4.1 s instead of 8.3 s; the dumps of the experiments of hctz, dulaglutide and acetaminophen (`pkpdsim-verify`, for acetaminophen with the reduced selections, its full dump runs out of memory with 0.9.6 as well) are identical to 0.9.6, byte for byte.

## Not changed

- **The models are compiled with MCJIT, not LLJIT** ([#331](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/issues/331)). roadrunner's LLJIT compiles the dulaglutide model of pkdb_models 6.9 times faster than its default MCJIT (1.5 s instead of 10.4 s, the initialization of its experiments in 2.6 s instead of 10.1 s), and the fits of hctz and dulaglutide at its optimization level NONE are the same as with MCJIT, bit for bit (the same evaluations and costs). But roadrunner 2.10 crashes with it: a process which compiles a model again while an earlier instance of it is alive dies with a segmentation fault in the compiled code of the model, in 3 of 8 fits of the dulaglutide model at the level NONE (the fits were not run at the other levels) and in 1 to 3 of 40 processes of a loop over its experiments at the levels NONE, LESS and AGGRESSIVE (DEFAULT was not run). MCJIT did not crash (0 of 6 fits, 0 of 20 processes of the loop). The higher optimization levels (LESS, DEFAULT, AGGRESSIVE) compile 4 times slower than NONE, do not make the simulations or the fits of hctz and dulaglutide faster and change the fits in the last bits. sbmlsim keeps MCJIT until roadrunner is fixed.
