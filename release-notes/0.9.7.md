# Release notes for sbmlsim 0.9.7
![sbmlsim](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/raw/develop/docs/images/favicon/sbmlsim-100x100-300dpi.png)

A performance release: the constant parameters and the species of a model are read once per instance of roadrunner instead of on every call of `sampling.parameters_of`. The simulations are unchanged. The faster JIT of roadrunner, LLJIT, was measured and is not used, roadrunner 2.10 crashes with it (see below).

## Performance

- **`parameters_of` reads the SBML of a model once** ([#332](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/issues/332)). `sampling.parameters_of` serialized the SBML of the instance of roadrunner and parsed it again on every call to list the constant parameters and the species, 30 ms per call on the acetaminophen model of pkdb_models (403 kB of SBML), whose experiments call it for 133 simulations of their sensitivity designs. The ids are now read once per instance of roadrunner and kept (`RoadrunnerSBMLModel.sbml_ids()`, read again for an instance which replaces `r`). The initialization of the experiments of acetaminophen (the test `test_experiments_initialize` of pkdb_models) takes 4.1 s instead of 8.3 s; the dumps of the experiments of hctz, acetaminophen and dulaglutide (`pkpdsim-verify`) are identical to 0.9.6.

## Not changed

- **The models are compiled with MCJIT, not LLJIT** ([#331](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/issues/331)). roadrunner's LLJIT compiles up to 6 times faster than its default MCJIT (the dulaglutide model of pkdb_models in 1.5 s instead of 10.3 s, the initialization of its experiments in 2.6 s instead of 10.1 s) with the same results bit for bit at its optimization level NONE, but roadrunner 2.10 crashes with it: a process which compiles a model again while an earlier instance of it is alive dies with a segmentation fault in the compiled code of the model, in 2 of 6 fits of the dulaglutide model and in 1 to 3 of 40 runs of its experiments, at every optimization level. The higher optimization levels (LESS, DEFAULT, AGGRESSIVE) compile 4 times slower than NONE, do not make the simulations or the fits of hctz and dulaglutide faster and change the results in the last bits. sbmlsim keeps MCJIT until roadrunner is fixed.
