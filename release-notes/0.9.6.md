# Release notes for sbmlsim 0.9.6
![sbmlsim](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/raw/develop/docs/images/favicon/sbmlsim-100x100-300dpi.png)

A release of one feature: one legend per row, right of the grid, with the entries of all plots of the row. A figure which does not opt in does not change.

## Features

- **One legend per row** ([#327](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/issues/327)). The new setting `legend_scope` of `FigureSettings` says which plots a legend collects: `"plot"` (the default) a legend per plot, as before, and `"row"` one legend per row of the grid. A figure whose plots of a row draw the same tasks wanted one legend per row, which 0.9.5 could only emulate by showing the legend of the last plot of a row: the entries of the other plots of the row were missing (in the band figures of the acetaminophen model of pkdb_models the study data and the dashed sums, drawn in the other columns) and a row whose last plot was empty had no legend; pkdb_models carried the workaround in 29 copies. The legend of a row now has the entries of all plots of the row, in their order, and shows an entry which reads and looks the same as an earlier one (its label, colours, line, marker, hatch and error bars), e.g. the simulation of every plot, once, the rule by which the interactive figures already merge their entries; entries with the same label which look different stay apart. The legends of the rows are right of the grid, one below the other at the top of their row, also when the last cell of a row is empty or has no plot: right of the right y axes and the colour bars of every plot of the last column, which gets the room of the widest legend, as a legend outside of 0.9.5 widens only the gap right of its plot; a legend taller than its row gives the row the room of its overflow below it. The `legend` of a plot decides whether its entries enter the legend of its row; `legend_position` and `legend_loc` apply to the legends of the plots only. As a setting, the scope cascades like the others: an experiment (`figure_settings`) or a run sets it for all of its figures, `Figure(..., settings={"legend_scope": "row"})` for one figure. The interactive figures of plotly give every row a legend of its own (`legend`, `legend2`, ...) at the top of the row, which scrolls when it is taller than its row and leaves out the entries of a plot without a legend. A figure with the scope `"plot"` is pixel-identical to 0.9.5 and its interactive version identical, checked on every figure which the tests and the examples render: 173 images and 48 interactive figures of the tests, 35 images and 17 SVG files of the examples, among them legends inside and outside, colour bars, right y axes, bands, tall legends and titles.
