# Release notes for sbmlsim 0.28.0
![sbmlsim](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/raw/develop/docs/images/favicon/sbmlsim-100x100-300dpi.png)

A panel repeats a dose: a dosing protocol gives the dose of a knob again at an interval for a number of days, once, twice or three times daily, as changes of the simulation which leave the model as it is. A page of an app shows its panels small, many readouts side by side, and a framework gives every model without panels a default app of its own, which pkpdsim builds from the doses, the diseases and the scans of a model.

## Features

- **Dosing protocols of a panel** ([#537](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/pull/537)). `PanelSimulation.dosing`, `PanelDosing(target, dose, interval, duration)`: the knob `dose` gives the first dose on its target, and the same dose is added to the target again at `dose_times(interval, duration, end)`, `k * interval` before the duration and the end (`MAX_DOSES`), the knobs of the times without a target and in a time unit (`time_factor`). The service gives the doses as changes `"<target> + <dose>"` of the simulation, a simulation per point of a grid whose doses differ by the point, the bundle `panelbundle/1` as `run.dosing`, which sbmlsim-core 0.6.0 gives as the increments of its changes, so a model is dosed by any of its routes (`PODOSE_<s>`, `IVDOSE_<s>`, ...) without a change of the model. The annotation of the SBML carries it (`<dosing target dose interval duration/>`), and the HCTZ demo has the page `dosing`.
- **The size of the panels of a page** ([#537](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/pull/537)). `AppPage.size`, `regular` or `small`, a page of many readouts side by side, which sbmlsim-web 0.3.0 draws four to a row.
- **The default app of a framework** ([#537](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/pull/537)). `Project.default_app(sbml)` gives the panels and the app of the web app of a model which declares neither, by default the panels of `Project.default_panels` and no app; pkpdsim overrides it with the pages of the doses and dosing protocols, the diseases and the scans of a model.
- **The web app runs the dosing protocols and the small pages**: `sbmlsim-core` 0.6.0 or newer and the vendored `sbmlsim-web` 0.3.0.
