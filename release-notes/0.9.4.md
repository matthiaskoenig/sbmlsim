# Release notes for sbmlsim 0.9.4
![sbmlsim](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/raw/develop/docs/images/favicon/sbmlsim-100x100-300dpi.png)

A patch release of fixes: the size of a compartment can be changed with a species of an assignment rule in it, which stopped every scan of the body weight of a model with such a species, and the legend of a band has one entry per band, its area in its own colour.

## Fixes

- **A compartment with a species of an assignment rule in it can be changed** ([#300](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/issues/300), [#301](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/pull/301)). A compartment keeps the concentrations of its species (PEtab v2): the pre-initialization and a change at a time set the concentration of every concentration species in it again after the compartment, also of a species of an assignment rule, which roadrunner refuses to set (`Could not set value for NON conserved moiety floating species ..., it is defined by an assignment rule`). A change of such a compartment, a scan of it and a scan of a parameter its initial assignment reads, e.g. the body weight, failed (0.9.0 to 0.9.3). A species of an assignment rule now follows its rule; every other species keeps its concentration (a concentration species) or its amount (an amount species) as before. In the HCTZ model of pkdb_models the species `hctz` of the extracellular fluid is such a species in a compartment which follows the body weight, so every sensitivity analysis of the model failed at the reference point of its design.
- **An initialization which fails does not break the model** ([#300](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/issues/300), [#301](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/pull/301)). The next initialization of a model which was not simulated restores the values the last one replaced. A value was recorded before it was set, so a value which roadrunner refused to set was restored by every later initialization of the loaded model, which failed on it as well: every later point of a scan on the model failed, also a plain simulation. A value is now recorded once it is replaced.
- **`parameters_of` leaves out the species of an assignment rule** ([#300](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/issues/300), [#301](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/pull/301)). `sampling.parameters_of(model, species=True)` listed them, and a design of all parameters and species stopped at the compile of its dimension, since a change cannot set such a species.
- **One legend entry per band, its area in its colour** ([#302](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/issues/302), [#301](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/pull/301)). The legend of a band had two entries, its median (or its upper boundary without a median, a thin line) and a grey area of its quantile range, e.g. `0-100 %`, once per panel and range, whatever the colour of the band, so two bands of different colours could not be told apart in the legend. A band now has one entry, its area in its own colour, labelled `<name> (<low>-<high> %)`, e.g. `C (5-95 %)`; a band per point of `over` has an entry per point, `<name>, <point label> (<low>-<high> %)`, and a band with a colour bar one entry of its name in the middle colour of the colour map. matplotlib and plotly draw the same entries.
