# Release notes for sbmlsim 0.47.0
![sbmlsim](https://github.com/matthiaskoenig/sbmlsim/raw/develop/docs/images/favicon/sbmlsim-100x100-300dpi.png)

The chart builder draws the figures of the second wave of pkdb_models as matplotlib draws them, on sbmlsim-core 0.33.0: error bars of two sides, contours, arrows, inset plots, coloured frames, stacked bars, rotated and boxed texts, several legends per panel.

## Features

- **Error bars of two sides** (#686): `xerr`, `yerr` of `curve` and `err` of `bar` take `(low, high)` or `{"low": ..., "high": ...}`, a value which is not finite a side which is not drawn.
- **`ChartPlot.contour(x, y, values, levels, filled=, scale=, low=, high=, center=, log=, gamma=, labels=, label_size=)`**: contour lines with labels inline (`labels=True` writes the levels `%g`) or filled bands; the scale spans the levels by default, as matplotlib's norm of a `ContourSet`.
- **`ChartPlot.arrow(x, y, x_from, y_from, head=, head_length=, head_width=, body=, shrink=, text=, ...)`**: matplotlib's `annotate`.
- **Texts**: `rotation`, `baseline`, `box`, `loc` (x and y `None`), `coordinates`, `clip`; `curve` and `polygon` take `coordinates` and `clip` too.
- **Plots**: `x2` (a top axis), `frame_color`, `frame_width`, `inset=(host, (x, y, width, height))`, `legend_anchor`, `legends`, `legend_tuples`.
- **Axes**: `ChartAxis(tick_label_color=)`, one colour or one per tick or category.
- **Stacks and boxes**: `base` of `bar` and `histogram`, `line`/`width` of a histogram (the edges of its bars), `median_color` and `whisker_opacity` of `box`.
- **Colour bars**: `extend`, `rows`, `gamma`, the placements `strip` and `panel`; `heatmap(gamma=)`.
- **Chart**: `legends` of the figure (`right`, `bottom`), `row_titles`, the fonts `plot_title_weight`, `colorbar_label`, `legend_family`.
- **Markers**: `octagon`, `tri_down`, `tri_up`, `tri_left`, `tri_right` in `MARKERS`.

## Changes

- **sbmlsim-core 0.33.0** (#686). Its log axes have the ticks, minor ticks and labels of matplotlib, a legend at `best` goes where matplotlib puts it, histograms and heatmaps have no seams, and a grid of thin rows keeps its layout.
